## §G The glue — what the legs jointly establish along each row of `A`

⚑ **CARRIED TESTIMONY, NOT ADJUDICATION.** Each entry says what the row's legs establish *together*
and, where they diverge, both positions with their denominators. Where GC and the dispatcher's
declared thesis agree, the agreement is weighted at ONE witness, per the brief's r3.

**G1 (from A1) — the question was mis-shaped, and the legs answered the better one.** Five trees,
five refusals of the binary, and five *different* third forms (§A-near). What they share is not a
mechanism but a decomposition: **the axis the brief named is about the POPULATION (how members are
found), and a second axis runs orthogonal to it (how a member is TRUSTED once found).** GB-1a
names it; LS's discovered-then-frozen, RD's declared-then-witnessed and CO's per-registry split are
each a point in that two-axis plane, and GC's computed routes are a third population mechanism the
binary cannot place. *The census's subject question should be re-asked on two axes.*

**G2 (from A2 + A5) — declaration's cost is paid late and silently unless something compares it to
the tree.** Four legs hold a stale-declaration instance from their own tree. Three (GB, CO, RD)
shipped the drift silently and found it later by a second reading; one (LS) caught it at the gate,
three times, at the cost of a generator per roster. RD-3 states the joint finding in one line:
*"a declaration's cost is not paid when it is written; it is paid when the tree moves and nothing
compares them."* ⚑ The legs establish the COST, not that the cost is decisive — LS paid it and
says the trade was right for its requirement.

**G3 (from A3 + A4) — the defect class every leg met is "two counts that agree by accident".**
Enrolment vs execution (CO, RD), modules vs fragments (GC), a disjunction's count vs per-member
counts (GB), a total vs a partition (RD). The joint discipline, stated by RD and practised by all
four: *print the terms, not the result* — a pass line names what RAN, an assertion prints its
operands. ⚑ This row is the most transferable output of the census and it is not about registries
at all; it is about how a registry's health is *reported*.

**G4 (from A5 + A6) — the walk's honest requirement is single-sourcing, and its honest hazard is
untested tolerance.** Two legs (GC, CO) reach the same requirement independently: the name and its
implementation must be impossible to separate, so there is no second place for drift to start. Two
legs (GC, GB) reach the same OPEN question independently: a walk tolerates a registry mid-edit, and
neither party can say whether that is a feature or the absence of a gate. ⚑ **GC's Q2 is the only
place its evidence runs against its declared bias, and it does** — laziness was found achievable in
a declaration, and the layout was found NOT to have caused the walk. Weighted accordingly.

**G5 (from A7) — the census itself produced re-derivations, and two legs filed them against their
own authors.** GC wrote a third reader over its own association; GB wrote a second walk over a
population its package already loads with validation, and measured four defects the shipped loader
forecloses. Joint finding, in GC's words: *every party filing this census needs the reconciliation
question answered about its own tree, and if each writes its own reader the apex cannot glue the
denominators.* ⚑ **A reconciliation reader that the engine carries is the one item this census can
act on** — nominated here, owner unassigned.

**G6 (from A8) — paperkit's `routes.py` is present in one tree (GC, vendored) and absent with a
control in four.** The normative arm's precondition — that the consumer *has* the owner's mechanism
to derive from — holds for one of five legs. ⚑ **The rule cannot be audited against trees it does
not reach**; LS states this exactly and refuses to infer. The glue records that the rule's reach is
one-in-five among filed legs and does not extend that to a claim about the rule.

**G7 (from A9 + A10) — the census's own method held.** Two legs measured the brief's revision
independently with byte-identical figures; five legs carried a control on every negative; two
recorded a control that overturned their first explanation before filing. *The instrument worked
